# 5-minute competition demo script

## Opening
“FloodWatch AI Geo begins with Thaton, Mon State: a focused local pilot designed to scale across ASEAN. The platform keeps predicted risk separate from observed inundation, then connects geospatial evidence to last-mile telecommunications.”

## Show the local replay
1. Open the Thaton Flood Intelligence Lab.
2. Point to the synthetic historical-replay banner.
3. Show the risk timeline rising at ten-minute decision intervals.
4. Switch from Risk to Inundation.
5. Explain source age and evidence quality.
6. Show the contribution bars explaining why the score changed.

## Show community impact
“Flood polygons become useful when intersected with roads, health sites, shelters, people and telecom coverage. The counts here are demo values; the production pipeline accepts validated local layers.”

## Show trusted alerting
Open the CAP review modal. Emphasize that the AI drafts; an authorized human approves. The delivery panel records dashboard delivery, simulated SMS, and the need for operator integration for Cell Broadcast.

## ASEAN close
“Local proof, regional design: the same model contract, country configuration, evidence ledger and alert workflow support all 11 ASEAN Member States, while country-held-out evaluation exposes where models transfer and where local fine-tuning is required.”
