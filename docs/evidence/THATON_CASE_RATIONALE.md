# Why Thaton, Myanmar is the flagship pilot

## Evidence-backed rationale
- ThinkHazard classifies both river-flood and urban-flood hazard in Thaton as medium using the modeled information available to that tool. The source also says exact locations should be checked because hazard may vary over short distances.
- MIMU has historical flood mapping for Myanmar and states that its updated mapping chronicles flood-affected townships from 2008 to 2025.
- ReliefWeb search records include flood-inundation or probable-flood maps mentioning Thaton in 2015, September 2024, and July 2025. These are candidate source materials for a historical replay, subject to checking licenses, map methodology and exact geometry.
- A 2019 DRR mission report for Kayin and Mon State recommended using open data to improve early warning and begin flood hazard/risk mapping, strengthening data interpretation capacity, collaboration, and decision-support systems.

## Competition framing
Thaton is the local proof-of-value. The regional architecture remains configured for all 11 ASEAN Member States. The project demonstrates telecommunications relevance by analyzing connectivity and preparing geo-targeted CAP test alerts, consistent with the competition's requirement that submissions address a telecommunications-domain challenge.

## Claims not to make
- Do not call synthetic polygons real current floods.
- Do not claim 10-minute satellite-confirmed inundation.
- Do not present synthetic exposure counts as official statistics.
- Do not claim a public Cell Broadcast was sent.
